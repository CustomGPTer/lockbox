What LockBox does not do

LockBox has no cloud sync, no private browser, no decoy vault with a second PIN, and no way to hide other apps inside it. It is not on Google Play, so you install it from this site, and because it cannot go online it shows the download page instead of updating itself.

Where the others do more

Keepsafe's listing describes syncing across devices and a decoy vault with its own PIN. The Calculator - photo vault listing describes a private browser and a fake vault with a different password. Both are on Google Play.
